A plain-language guide to sports betting math

The Fine Print

Odds are written to sound like tips. They're actually just math — and once you can read it, nobody can talk you into a bad number. Built for people picking this up for the first time.

What does −150 actually mean?

Enter American odds (the number you'll see on any sportsbook) to see the implied probability and what you'd need to win to break even.

The hidden cut

Sportsbooks price both sides of a bet so that the probabilities add up to more than 100%. That extra bit is the "vig" — the house's built-in profit margin. Enter both sides of a real matchup to see it.

Why parlays feel better than they are

Stack a few "likely" bets into a parlay and your odds of winning the whole thing drop faster than most people expect. Add legs to watch it happen.
